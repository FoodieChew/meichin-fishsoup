What Makes Us Special

Unlike modern variations, our classic Teochew-style fish soup is incredibly "Cheng" (clear) yet deeply robust and refreshing. Our fish slices undergo a special hand-cut technique to stay velvety-smooth, soft, yet perfectly firm.
